AUTO LOOP 0.7.1 — TEST SCOPE

Run the built-in checks with:  "Auto Loop.exe" --self-test <empty folder>
Results are written to internal-tests.txt in that folder. 76 checks pass; the
current list is in AUTOMATED-TEST-RESULTS.txt. Self-tests and previews never
read or change your settings.xml.

WHAT THE CHECKS COVER
- Input encoding: INPUT structure size, key and mouse flags, virtual-desktop
  coordinates.
- Engine timing: exact repeat counts, intervals, press duration, delayed ticks,
  time limits, Test once, and the Stop after rules.
- Safety: held keys and buttons are released on stop, pause and focus loss;
  failed releases are retried; playback won't start without a global Stop
  shortcut; input failures stop playback.
- Selected app: playback pauses instead of clicking outside the target app;
  clicks after a fixed-position move are checked at the intended point;
  administrator targets are refused with an explanation.
- Recording: physical vs injected events, press/release pairs, drags,
  double-clicks, held keys at the end of a recording, the step limit, clicks on
  Auto Loop's own windows being ignored, presses outside the selected app being
  ignored, and the keys or clicks that switch away from the app being trimmed.
- Pick app / Pick spot: the picked window or spot is used, the picking click
  and its release never reach the app, Esc and right-click cancel, a click on
  Auto Loop cancels, and picking explains itself if the hooks are missing.
- Status bar, countdown, tray and notice: the text for running, paused,
  waiting and recording; the countdown and tray follow the countdown; the
  "finished" notice text, and no notice for Test once or a manual Stop.
- Overlays on screen: the status bar, countdown and spot dot are really shown;
  none becomes the active window, the countdown and dot let clicks through,
  the bar can be clicked and moves out of the way.
- Hints: interval and Stop after descriptions for Click, Double click, Keys
  and Record.
- 0.7.1: double-click timing and validation; key combinations pressed and
  released in order and never left held; exact shortcut conflicts only; scan
  codes on keys; Esc cancels the shortcut dialog but can be a repeated key;
  opening a routine reselects its app or explains it is closed; the PC is kept
  awake only while running; input hooks are needed only while recording or
  picking.
- Files: routine and settings round trip, remembered settings restored on the
  next start, invalid saved settings ignored, validation of invalid or
  unbalanced routines, and a visible message when settings can't be saved.
- Layout: control sizes, alignment and clipping at 100% scaling; the window is
  the same size as in 0.6.

Engine and recorder checks use a simulated clock and simulated hook events.
They send no input to the desktop.

LIVE CHECK
The built app was started, closed and started again: it showed one window
when idle (no status bar or tray icon), the interval hint, and "settings from
last time" on the second start.

DISPLAY SCALING
"Auto Loop.exe" --preview <folder> <scale> renders every view, the status bar
and the countdown at a given scale. Renders at 1 and 1.5 were checked by eye.
This simulates scaling on a 100% display; it has not been tried on real
150%/200% or mixed-DPI hardware.

NOT VERIFIED
- Live playback into real applications on other computers.
- How the "finished" notice looks with Windows notifications or Focus Assist
  turned off (Windows may hide it).
- Games, apps with anti-cheat, and remote-desktop sessions.
- Real mixed-DPI multi-monitor setups.
- Detection of administrator apps that Windows doesn't let normal apps query
  (Auto Loop then assumes the app isn't elevated).
